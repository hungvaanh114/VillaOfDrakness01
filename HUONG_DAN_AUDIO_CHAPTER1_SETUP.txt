  Goi bang: AudioManager.Instance.PlayDeathVoice(1..3)
  Da dung truc tiep: DEATH-03 trong TriggerDeath.
  DEATH-01 va DEATH-02 nen dat vao trigger mieng gieng truoc khi goi TriggerDeath.


4. GOI Y SETUP THEM TRONG UNITY

- Tao object HopNhac_BaLan trong Thu Phong.
  Khi player interact: goi AudioManager.Instance.PlayMusicBoxStartup(), sau do AudioManager.Instance.PlayBaLanTape().

- Tao trigger DocNhatKy trong Thu Phong.
  Sau khi hien noi dung nhat ky: goi PlayDiaryReaction(1), doi 1-2 giay goi PlayDiaryReaction(2), doi tiep goi PlayDiaryReaction(3).

- Tao trigger MiengGieng.
  Khi player toi gan: goi PlayDeathVoice(1).
  Khi nhin xuong gieng: goi PlayDeathVoice(2), goi PlayWellJumpscare(), sau do GameController.Instance.TriggerDeath().

- Neu muon thay clip UI/SFX:
  Mo Assets/MainGame/Resources/Audio/AudioData.asset va keo clip moi vao slot tuong ung.
